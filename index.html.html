WHAT IS SECRET BUDDY?

A Safe Space To Speak Freely

Secret Buddy is a digital support platform created for students who may want someone to talk to, guidance during difficult situations, or simply a place where they feel understood.

Instead of requiring users to reveal their real identity, Secret Buddy allows them to create an anonymous username and interact in a more comfortable environment.

Your identity can stay private while your voice can still be heard.
WHY WAS IT CREATED?

Because Student Life Can Be Difficult

Students experience different challenges, but many may hesitate to speak because they fear judgement or recognition.

📚

Student Pressure

Academic pressure, career uncertainty, relationships, competition and everyday challenges can become difficult to handle alone.

👀

Fear Of Judgement

Students may worry about being judged, misunderstood or recognised when sharing personal thoughts.

🤝

Need For Connection

Sometimes people simply need someone who can listen, understand and provide helpful guidance.
